About Agent Forge AI

Built for the one row that matters

We started Agent Forge AI because dashboards tell you something changed — never why.

Our origin

Built by engineers who were paged at 3am one too many times

Before Agent Forge AI, our founding team ran data infrastructure at three different high-growth startups. Every outage looked the same: a chart spiked, an alert fired, and someone spent an hour digging through logs to find a cause that a model could have surfaced in seconds. We built the tool we wished we'd had.

Our approach

Calm, confident software for high-stakes decisions

Analytics tools shouldn't feel like toys. Every interaction in Agent Forge AI is deliberately restrained — no flashy animations, no vanity metrics, just clear signal delivered the moment it matters, explained in language a human can act on immediately.

Values

What we optimize for

Explainability over automation

An alert without a reason is just noise. We ship explanations before we ship new triggers.

Restraint in design

Calm, confident motion — never flashy. If an animation doesn't communicate state, it doesn't ship.

Trust through transparency

Tenant-isolated models, full audit logs, and no shared training data — by default, for everyone.
