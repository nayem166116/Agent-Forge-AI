Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

CODEGPT LLC ("Agent Forge AI", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use our website and platform (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

We collect account information you provide directly (name, work email, company), usage data from your interactions with the Service, and pipeline metadata you connect to Agent Forge AI for analysis. We do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to operate and improve the Service, provide customer support, send product and billing communications, and maintain the security of our platform. Pipeline data you connect is processed solely to generate the anomaly detection, forecasting, and reporting you request, and is never used to train models shared across customers.

3. Data retention

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active, plus a limited period afterward for legal and audit purposes. You may request deletion at any time as described below.

4. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. We honor requests under GDPR, CCPA, and equivalent regional frameworks.

5. Security

Agent Forge AI maintains SOC 2 Type II controls, encryption in transit and at rest, and tenant-isolated data storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work continuously to protect your data.

6. International transfers

We may process and store information in the United States and other countries where Agent Forge AI or its subprocessors operate, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via email or an in-product notice at least 14 days before taking effect.
